# Abrasion

[Back to the concepts](README.md) · [glossary](../glossary.md)

**What it means.** The cost a review puts on an honest provider: a wrongful pend, a wrongful adjustment or denial, and late payment.

**Why it exists.** Preventing overpayment is not free if it burdens honest providers. Without this term a strategy could look good by pending everything.

**Example.** A pend of a correctly billed claim costs $43.84; a wrongful denial costs half of $200 plus appeal handling in expectation.

**Where to find it.** [score P1.0](../score_prepay.md)
