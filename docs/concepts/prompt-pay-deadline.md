# Prompt-pay deadline

[Back to the concepts](README.md) · [glossary](../glossary.md)

**What it means.** The date by which a clean claim must be paid under state law or contract; paying later costs interest.

**Why it exists.** Pending delays payment. The deadline turns that delay into a dollar cost, charged only on claims that turned out to be correctly billed.

**Example.** A claim pended for 45 days is 15 days late on a 30-day clock; interest on its paid amount at 18% a year is charged as abrasion.

**Where to find it.** [score P1.0](../score_prepay.md), parameter `prompt_pay_days` (an assumption: real clocks vary by state and contract)
