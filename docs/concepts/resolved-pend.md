# Resolved pend

[Back to the concepts](README.md) · [glossary](../glossary.md)

**What it means.** A pend that returned something a coder can read: a chart inside the wait limit, or a statement that no record exists.

**Why it exists.** A pend with no answer says nothing about the visit. Training on such pends as if the claim were honest (or upcoded) teaches the valuer's assumption; leaving them out lifted WP1.0 from about $6,000 to about $188,000 on ten select seeds.

**Example.** A provider never answers; the claim is denied at day 45, but the payer learned nothing about the visit.

**Where to find it.** `src/wp10.py` (`is_resolved`), [hypothesis loop](../wp10_loop.md)
