# Daily pend cap

[Back to the concepts](README.md) · [glossary](../glossary.md)

**What it means.** The most claims a strategy may pend on one receipt day, standing in for review staff capacity.

**Why it exists.** Staff are finite. The cap makes strategies choose which claims are worth a chart.

**Example.** Cap 10: of 300 claims received on a day, at most ten are pended.

**Where to find it.** `src/wp10.py` (`CAP_PER_DAY`), [score P1.0](../score_prepay.md)
