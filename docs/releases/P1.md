# Release P1 notes

[Back to the README](../../README.md) · [true test](../p1_test.md) · [reflection](../reflection_P1.md) · [versions](../versioning.md)

Released 2026-10-26. Prepay point 4 (pend and request records) on synthetic data 4.2, score P1.0.

**What it is.** For each incoming claim a strategy chooses to pay, to pend (request the medical record and decide from it), or to adjust. WP1.1 is the workflow: a provider suspicion score refreshed as claims arrive, a learned rule that estimates what pending each claim is worth, a daily cap of 10 pends and a $15 floor, then rules on the returned record. AP1.0 is a small model-driven agent over the same advice.

**Result.** On ten fresh test seeds WP1.1 beat the best claim edit that needs no records by $5,709 a seed (p = 0.002, nine of ten seeds, positive under every cost assumption tried). The agent scored $2,824 a seed below the workflow it mirrors. Almost all of the money is the generator's planted impossible-visit pattern; ordinary level upcoding is not caught profitably.

**What changed on the way.** The first workflow, WP1.0, passed one confirm and then missed its final development run; training the rule on resolved pends only and adding a $15 pend floor gave WP1.1, which passed a confirm, a final development run and the test. Cost of paid model calls: $0.30 (cap $1.00).

**Seeds.** P-DEV (60), P-DEV2 (60), P-CONFIRM j = 0 to 19, P-TEST j = 0 to 9 are used; P-RESERVED and the rest of P-CONFIRM and P-TEST are unread ([seeds](../seeds.md)).
